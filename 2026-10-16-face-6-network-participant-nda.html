FACE NETWORK PARTICIPANT NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Families Advocating for Campus Equality (FACE) values the importance, and makes every effort to ensure nondisclosure of Confidential Information1 received from persons who have contacted, been referred to or communicated with FACE Representatives for the purpose of support, advocacy or other FACE services. This Nondisclosure Agreement (Agreement) is entered into by and between Families Advocating for Campus Equality (FACE) as a nonprofit entity, and (Applicant), who has requested the permission to participate in the FACE Private Network, as defined below. The purpose of this Agreement is to confirm that those who wish to participate in the FACE Private Network commit to nondisclosure of any Confidential Information, as defined below, acquired through that participation.

The following definitions apply to this entire Agreement:

  1. FACE Representative: FACE executive officers, directors, board members, and part-time or full-time employees, volunteers, and interns.
  2. FACE Participant: persons who have contacted, been referred to, or volunteered on behalf of FACE, or otherwise participated in or benefited from FACE activities, or communicated with FACE Representatives or Participants for the purpose of providing or receiving advice, support, advocacy, or any other services connected with FACE activities and its Private Network.
  3. FACE Private Network: any FACE-administered method or other activity facilitating communication or contact between and among FACE Participants and/or FACE Representatives, which conveys Confidential Information, including, but not limited to, in-person, online or telephonic meetings, and other electronic formats such video conferences, emails, text messages, or online forums.
  4. Confidential Information: any information, whether or not it has been identified as "personal” or “confidential," communicated, provided or disclosed by any FACE Participant or Representative in the context of a FACE Private Network shall be presumed confidential and disclosure prohibited.
    1. Confidential Information may include, but is not limited to:
      1. Oral, written, online, telephonic, visual, or electronic communications, text messages and emails;
      2. Names of persons, schools and cities, photographic images, locations, and any other factual or personally identifiable information;
      3. Information related to FACE’s conduct of business including not otherwise disclosed financial and strategic information, and internal communications.
    2. Confidential Information does not include information which has been:
      1. Publicly known at the time of disclosure or became publicly known through no action(s) attributable to Applicant;
      2. Discovered or learned by through legitimate, alternative means, other than from a FACE Private Network, Participant, or Representative; or
      3. Divulged by a FACE Participant or Representative with express prior written approval of the FACE Participant or Representative who disclosed the information.

In accordance with the terms of this Agreement:

  1. Applicant agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information, unless and until one of the conditions in Section 4.b. above has been satisfied.
  2. In the event of a FACE Participant’s failure to comply with this Agreement, FACE reserves the right to terminate that FACE Participant’s access to all FACE Private Networks.
  3. Applicant acknowledges that FACE and FACE representatives are not responsible for disclosure of Confidential Information by other FACE Participants; the ability to ensure nondisclosure of Confidential Information by any person(s) is limited to that communicated to or received by FACE Representatives.
  4. If a court finds any provision of this Agreement invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement shall be interpreted so as best to effect the intent of the parties.
  5. This Agreement expresses the complete understanding of the parties and supersedes any prior agreements or representations, and may not be amended except in writing by both parties
  6. The failure to exercise any right provided in this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of prior or subsequent rights.
  7. Applicant acknowledges that a violation of the terms of this Agreement may cause damage and harm to FACE, FACE Participants, or FACE Representatives and that any such damage or harm will be difficult if not impossible to calculate in monetary terms and may be irreparable to FACE. Applicant agrees that, upon notice from FACE declaring a breach of this Agreement, Applicant shall immediately cease all further activities which are, or are claimed by FACE to be, a breach of this Agreement and shall immediately return to FACE any and all written or other tangible material containing any FACE Information in Applicant’s possession and shall not keep any copies thereof. FACE may also avail itself of any other remedies available by law.
  8. This Agreement will be governed by the laws of the state of Ohio without regard to its conflicts of laws principles.
  9. Applicant hereby expressly consents to the personal jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the state of Ohio for any lawsuit filed there against Applicant by FACE concerning Applicant’s participation or the termination of Applicant’s participation or arising from or relating to this Agreement.

Applicant acknowledges and agrees to each of the following items:

  1. (i) I am executing this Agreement voluntarily and without any duress or undue influence by Families Advocating for Campus Equality or anyone else.
  2. (ii) I have carefully read this agreement. I have had the opportunity to ask any questions needed for me to understand the terms, consequences and binding effect of this agreement, and fully understand them.
  3. (iii) I am aware of my right to seek legal advice from an attorney of my choice prior to signing this agreement.
