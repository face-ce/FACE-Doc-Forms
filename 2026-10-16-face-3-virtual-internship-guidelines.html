Guidelines for Virtual Internship

Virtual internships, sometimes referred to as remote-based positions, are internships in which an individual works in a location other than a traditional office setting. Generally this means working from home or somewhere close to home. During remote internships, much of the communication between the organization and their intern(s) takes place via methods such as video calling, telephone and email. What differentiates virtual internships from traditional internships is that the projects interns work on does not take place in a conventional office location.

This is an independent work environment which necessitates a high level of self-motivation and personal organizational skills. If you ever feel disconnected from the organization's overall mission and goals, do not hesitate to schedule a phone call, Zoom meeting, or email conversation with your supervisor for assistance in getting back on track.

Although a key feature of remote internships is the flexibility of the work schedule, supervisors and interns should agree upon a definitive time allotment per week and per day for internship activities. Interns will not intern for more than 8 hours a day, will take all required breaks and lunches and won’t intern more than 40 hours a week. All projects/tasks will be done during your scheduled hours.

Due to this internship being virtual, it is a requirement that you have a computer, phone, and access to the internet. If any issues arise regarding your internet access, computer, or phone, please let your supervisor know as soon as possible so that you can make arrangements to reschedule your work.

Communication with your supervisor is key to a successful virtual internship. Supervisors will work with the intern at the beginning of the internship to develop a plan to ensure that the established goals are met, and check in regularly throughout the internship to see if the intern is on track to meet these goals. Interns are required to report regularly, preferably in writing, on their progress for each project/task.
