PURPOSE OF THE INTERNSHIP

The purpose of this unpaid internship program is to assist the intern with gaining work experience. While participating in ongoing projects and activities related to the everyday work of FACE, the intern will be provided with conditions for work experience and professional development during the length of the internship.

An Internship with Families Advocating for Campus Equality gives individuals the opportunity to combine the knowledge acquired from school with real hands-on work experience. Interns can make new connections, gain confidence, and add to their resume.

CONDITIONS OF THE INTERNSHIP

  • This internship is aimed toward the intern’s professional development.
  • The Intern will receive guidance from their immediate supervisor and will be assigned tasks aimed at the development of the intern’s skills and qualifications related to the regular work that is done at FACE.
  • The internship is related to work experience and there is no guarantee or expectation that the experience gained by the intern will secure subsequent employment with FACE upon completion of the internship.
  • The Intern does not replace or displace any employee of the organization.
  • The Intern will receive direct supervision by a supervisor.
  • The organization does not derive an advantage from the activities performed by the Intern.

  • The intern is not entitled to insurance coverage and FACE is not liable to any health conditions and injuries that may arise for the unpaid intern during the internship period.
